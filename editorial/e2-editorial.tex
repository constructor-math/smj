\noindent Dear reader,

\vspace{0.25cm}

It is with great pleasure that we present the inaugural edition of the
{\color{smjcrimson}\textit{Student Mathematical Journal}}, on behalf of the
Constructor University Mathematics Society.

\vspace{0.25cm}

This journal was born from a desire to give undergraduate researchers a venue
to communicate their work and to foster a culture of mathematical inquiry at
Constructor University Bremen. Each article collected here originated in our
{\color{smjgold}\emph{Mathematical Undergraduate Seminar}} (\UgS) and has been developed into an expository
piece intended to be accessible to a wide audience.

\vspace{0.25cm}

In its very-first volume, the \SMJ proudly presents to you
five excellent articles spanning quantum computation, information theory, 
differential geometry, and algebraic geometry. Most remarkably, the authors
hail from mathematics, physics, computer science, and electrical engineering. As I sign-off as Editor-in-Chief, I am excited to see how this journal evolves in
the coming years. 

\vspace{0.25cm}


\textbf{To the students}, I encourage you to get involved in this exciting venture
that is the \UgS. There you will always find a place to be curious, to ask questions, to make mistakes 
and learn from them, to grow as a mathematician, and most importantly, as a person. 

\vspace{0.25cm}

\textbf{To the seminarists}, I invite you to pass on the torch to the next generation
of students, to explore fascinating mathematics through the \UgS, to share that same fascination
through the \SMJ. 

\vspace{0.25cm}

\textbf{To Ahmed Maghri {\color{smjgray}CS'27}}, you entered the \SMJ as a seminarist, and now you succeed me as the Editor-in-Chief. In between, I have watched you grow into the brilliant mathematician and 
capable leader that you are today. I trust you to uphold what came before and forge what comes next.

\vspace{0.25cm}

The \SMJ is a testament to the mathematical excellence of those who
contributed to it, and to the potential of those courageous enough to try. 
May it transcend the brevity of our time and echo softly in the spaces we leave behind. $\boxed \xi$

\vspace{0.4cm}

\noindent\textit{Yours Sincerely, }\\
\textbf{Omar Elshinawy --- MATH'25}\\
Editor-in-Chief of the \SMJ
