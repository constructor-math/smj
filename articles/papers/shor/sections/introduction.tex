\section{Introduction}

This article takes the reader on a journey through Shor's algorithm. This
journey will be structured as follows: We will start with introducing what
is cryptography itself, explaining its goal, RSA, and a brief introduction
to the algorithm we will spend most time on, Shor's algorithm.
We will then establish some essential background on quantum computing, so that
the reader is able to follow the details of the explained algorithm.
And finally, we will move to going over all of Shor's Algorithm, and how it
is supposed to break RSA.
