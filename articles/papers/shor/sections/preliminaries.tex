\section{A Note on Prerequisites}

This article assumes no prior knowledge of quantum computing or advanced
mathematics. Basic familiarity with the following concepts will be helpful:
binary-to-decimal conversion (e.g., $101_2 = 4 + 0 + 1 = 5$), elementary
complex numbers ($i^2 = -1$), and matrix operations such as transpose and
complex conjugate. Any other concepts are introduced as they appear. Readers
who wish to go deeper on any topic are encouraged to consult the references
at the end of the article.
