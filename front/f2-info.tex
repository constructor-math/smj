  \smjsubheading{The Journal}

  \vspace{\internal}

  The \textit{Student Mathematical Journal} (\SMJ) is the
  undergraduate mathematics journal of\\ 
  Constructor University Bremen, published\\
  annually by the Mathematics Society at\\
  Constructor University (\MSCU).

  \vspace{\internal}

  The \SMJ publishes expository and research articles written by undergraduate students. All articles originate in the \MSCU's own Mathematics
  Undergraduate Seminar (\UgS) and are developed into self-contained\\
  expositions, accessible to a broad\\ mathematical audience.

  \smjsubheading{Submission Guidelines}

  \vspace{\internal}

  We welcome submissions from students at\\
  Constructor University \& visiting students\\
  who have participated in the Mathematical\\
  Undergraduate Seminar (\UgS).

  \vspace{\internal}
  
  Each paper is reviewed by two \UgS\\
  seminarists,  or, by a faculty advisor when\\
  appropriate. Authors iterate with reviewers\\
  until the manuscript is suitable for final\\
  typesetting.

  \vspace{\internal}

  To participate in the \UgS, there is an\\
  application process. One simply attends to a\\
  member of the \MSCU board, possible\\
  through the respective contact channels, or\\
  closely follows the \MSCU's latest\\
  announcements.

  \vspace{\external}

  \smjsubheading{Recorded Talks}

  \vspace{\internal}

  Each article in the {\color{smjcrimson}{\textbf{SMJ}}} is accompanied by a recorded talk delivered
  at the \UgS. The recordings are freely
  available and provide an accessible entry point to each paper's ideas.
